# LABB Studio

Website for LABB Studio, a photo-and-film studio for rent in Bielsko-Biała. These terms describe what the studio offers to the people who rent it.

## Equipment

**Equipment Item**:
One product type the studio provides, shown as one slide with one Cutout and one set of details. Identical units are a single Equipment Item with a quantity.
_Avoid_: Slide, photo, gear piece

**Included Equipment**:
The Equipment Items a renter can use at no extra cost, because they are covered by the studio rental (*w cenie wynajmu*).
_Avoid_: Free equipment, base equipment

**Additional Equipment**:
Equipment beyond the Included Equipment that a renter can request in advance, and which may cost extra.
_Avoid_: Paid equipment, extra gear

**Cutout**:
The background-free image of one Equipment Item. Anything in the image that belongs to a different Equipment Item is greyed out, so it is clear which item the slide is about.
_Avoid_: Photo, stripped PNG

## Finding the studio

**Route Video**:
The silent film of the walk to the studio door, shown so renters can recognise the way. It is the first thing a visitor sees on the directions page.
_Avoid_: Studio video, tour, clip

**Route Scheme**:
The annotated aerial image with the route to the studio drawn on it (*dojazd*).
_Avoid_: Small map, map image

**Location Map**:
The interactive Google map showing where the studio is. It shows the place, not the route to the door.
_Avoid_: Big map
